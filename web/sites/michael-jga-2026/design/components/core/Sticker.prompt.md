Glowing neon doodle used to break up a section edge — decorative only, always `aria-hidden`.

```jsx
<Sticker glyph="♛" tone="gold" size="lg" style={{ top: "-1.2rem", right: "8%" }} />
```

- Absolutely positioned: the parent needs `position:relative`.
- Two or three per page, never a row of them; never as a functional icon.
- `cyan` is the groom's special accent — reserve it.
