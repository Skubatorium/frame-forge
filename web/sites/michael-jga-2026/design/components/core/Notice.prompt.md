Low-key advisory box — used for the "please don't share the link" note and bandwidth warnings.

```jsx
<Notice mark="!"><p>Privat. Bitte den Link nicht weitergeben — Zugang gibt es auf Nachfrage.</p></Notice>
```

- `quiet` by default; `loud` (gold) only when the reader must act.
- Body text stays Poppins at `--step--1`; no display font inside.
