This was the first trip with a drone at all. The thought of somehow capturing this landscape was there throughout the planning - and for fjords, plateaus and waterfalls the view from above is simply the right one.

So I read my way into the subject and settled on a DJI Lito X1: large sensor, plenty of safety systems, a fantastic piece of kit. The first flights were tentative, but that passed quickly - within a few days the shots became usable and then genuinely good.

The film is cut just as calmly. Long takes, slow movements, two pieces of music flowing into one another. The order is broadly chronological, but that isn't the point here: what counts are the shots, not the legs of the trip.

About the film

78 pure drone shots, laid end to end: the cloud layer hanging in the valley at the Trollstigen, releasing the view only in strips. Waterfalls coming out of the rock face and disappearing into the depth. The unreal turquoise of the fjords and glacial rivers, fed by the ice up on the plateau. Mirror-smooth forest lakes in the evening, the bare expanse of Valdresflye, and finally the flat, clear skerry coast.

No captions, no place names. If you want to know where something is, you'll find it in the Roadtrip Edition or on the facts page.

Music: Naturaleza (Mose Edit) (Mose & Danit) and Cuatro Vientos (Danit). The two tracks provide the only structure and run into each other without a hard cut.
