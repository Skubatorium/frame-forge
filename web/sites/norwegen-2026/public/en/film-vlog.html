Roadtrip Edition

This film is the journey itself: chronological, with date, place and leg - a travel diary of eighteen days.

It starts at home in Grevenbroich, with an estate car whose roof box is still open, and ends on the same driveway. In between: the ferry across the Skagerrak, quiet days by the lake in Telemark, the pass road down to the Aurlandsfjord, twenty kilometres of darkness in the Lærdal tunnel, the waterfall near Olden, the Seven Sisters in the Geirangerfjord, the eleven hairpin bends of the Trollstigen in the mist, and the bare expanse of Valdresflye.

Three pieces of music divide the film into three parts, each blending into the next. There is text only where it helps: date, place, distance for the day. There is no commentary.

About the film
