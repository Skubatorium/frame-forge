On accuracyThe total of 3,829 km comes from the stage data; small local trips (shopping, day trips) are not all included, so the real figure is a little higher. The circuit from 23 July to 1 August, starting and ending in Skien, measures 1,332 km at 22 h 40 min of pure driving time. The lowest point of the trip is sea level along the fjord and coastal legs; the start in Grevenbroich sits at about 40 m.

The drone

The first trip with a drone at all. The wish to capture this landscape from the air was there before we set off - what came of it was a DJI Lito X1, chosen for its large sensor and its safety systems.

The learning happened on the road, and quickly. In the end the footage found its place in both films - the Drone Edition consists of nothing else.

Curiosities

Numbers that appear in no travel summary and stuck with us anyway.

Reviewed: 465 video clips running almost four hours, and 716 photos. 167 and 78 shots respectively made it into the films.

Caught on the skerry coast near Langesund, by the six-year-old, tiny. The only one of the whole trip.

20km inside rock

In one go beneath the mountain: the Lærdal tunnel. It produced the only photo of the trip without GPS data - no reception.

26,900metres of climb

Cumulative ascent over the whole distance - three times up Mount Everest, only more comfortably. (Derived from the GPS elevation profile, not an odometer reading.)

1friendship
